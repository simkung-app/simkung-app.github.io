# Simkung Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 27, 2026

Simkung ("the app") is made by Eric Yang, an independent developer based in Canada. This policy explains what information the app handles. The short version: the app works offline, has no accounts, and does not collect personal information.

## Information we do not collect

- No accounts, names, email addresses or phone numbers.
- No contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera access.
- No advertising, ad tracking or tracking across other apps and websites.
- No analytics or usage tracking.

## Information stored on your device

The app saves your learning progress, your custom collections and your settings (such as the word packs you picked and whether romanization is shown) on your device, in storage shared between the app and its widgets. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

Pronunciation audio is generated on your device by Apple's built-in text-to-speech. No audio or text is sent to us.

## Purchases and subscriptions

Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.

To check whether you have an active subscription and to let you restore purchases, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription management service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and transaction information from Apple, such as which product you bought and when, along with basic technical details like your device's operating system version and App Store country. This information is not linked to your name or email by us. See RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Apple's handling of your App Store purchases is covered by Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will help.

## Your rights

Because we do not collect personal information, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have questions about your data, including under Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA), contact us below.

## Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling information differently (for example, by adding optional analytics), we will update this policy and change the effective date above before the change ships.
